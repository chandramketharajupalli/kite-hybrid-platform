Initial NOT_ENROLLED. Owner identity reference, identity-verification method, enrollment authority, trust-root fingerprint, public-key fingerprint, key-provider identity, enrollment timestamp, expiry, revocation endpoint/ledger and independent approval evidence are null. Permitted governance roles are proposed EVIDENCE_ADOPTION_OWNER only, not granted. Rotation procedure reference is this policy plus the separate custody/revocation policies; no actual rotation decision exists.

Obtain explicit approval for real key generation/enrollment first. Independently verify original identity and role-delegation evidence, conflicts and enrollment-reviewer authority; keep sensitive originals in approved private custody. Approve crypto/provider provenance and non-exportability requirements. Establish the root independently of the submitted key. Bind public-key bytes/fingerprint, provider, role, permitted decisions, effective/expiry bounds and revocation authority to independently attributable approval evidence. A signature or proof of possession alone cannot establish identity or role.

Require independently justified trusted time, fresh revocation, durable replay ledger and externally anchored rollback/recovery certification before real acceptance. Challenge-response and signature vectors must use an approved provider. No synthetic key, TEST_ONLY decision, local JSON approval, matching hash or passing test may be enrolled as real authority. A future decision must bind both immutable amendment hashes, exact fresh certificate and separately reconciled source baseline.

Revocation/rotation/root replacement/lost-key recovery need separate authenticated scope-bound decisions and immutable audit history. Never erase certified results. Current Phase14.44A adoption remains NOT_AUTHORIZED; Stage1, rollback execution, Seriesv2 and acquisition remain prohibited.
