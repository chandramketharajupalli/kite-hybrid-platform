# Descriptive synthetic metric policy v1

This is an analytics convention, not a qualification amendment or capital approval. Daily returns use completed session equity ratios; cumulative return uses original simulated capital. Intraday drawdown uses only recorded equity snapshots and recorded high-water observations. Incomplete sessions appear in the daily ledger but do not become completed-session statistical observations. No absent equity observations are interpolated.

Volatility is sample standard deviation of daily returns, annualized by sqrt(252). Sharpe uses average daily return divided by sample standard deviation times sqrt(252), with an explicit synthetic zero annual risk-free assumption. Sortino uses the square root of the mean squared negative daily returns as downside deviation. At least 30 completed sessions and a complete sequence are required. Calmar additionally requires at least 252 completed sessions, positive drawdown and positive compounded wealth; annualized compounded return is divided by maximum fractional drawdown. Undefined denominators and insufficient evidence produce NOT_COMPUTABLE and null values. Decimal precision uses the frozen research context; nonfinite inputs are rejected.

Closed-trade net values determine trade counts, win/loss/breakeven, average win/loss, expectancy and profit factor. Zero losses do not yield infinite profit factor. Gross P&L minus net P&L must equal costs; reused tariff quotes reconcile component totals. Turnover sums executed entry and exit notionals, including entries of unresolved positions. Average cost per completed trade excludes outstanding entry fees. Tariff configurations and hash are synthetic assumptions, not claims about actual Zerodha contract notes.

No separate reference-price slippage ledger exists in the accepted portfolio contract, so measured slippage is NOT_COMPUTABLE. Exposure-adjusted return is likewise unavailable without a time-weighted exposure contract. Recovery intervals retain actual observation timestamps; unresolved drawdowns have no fabricated recovery duration.

Qualification stays frozen: 70% overall observed win rate, 100 completed OOS trades, 30 trades per holdout fold, 60% per-fold win rate, positive expectancy, 10% drawdown, Wilson checks and Bonferroni family 13,380. Analytics serializes and hashes the existing policy. Synthetic observations never promote or disqualify genuine pairs. Two-session examples do not receive Sharpe or Sortino values.
