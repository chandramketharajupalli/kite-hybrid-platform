# Retention and deletion preparation

No retention permission has been verified. No genuine acquisition or storage is authorized. Maximum durations and approved storage destinations remain null for raw master CSV, transient parsed records, symbol/token mappings, mapping metadata, source hashes, timestamps, audit receipts, historical candles and derived outputs. Audit metadata is not assumed exempt from restrictions. Refresh permission would not establish historical-snapshot retention permission.

Before acquisition, an approved category-specific policy must bind source clause/hash, purpose, permitted location, maximum duration, processing rules, deletion triggers, deletion method and verification receipt, backup restrictions, restore handling and derived-output treatment. No automatic permanent database. Unknown duration fails closed. Expiry is checked at the exact boundary; expired inputs are rejected before research execution. Revocation, subscription termination where applicable and purpose completion must be assessed as deletion triggers from verified terms.

Proposed controls pending approval: restrict temporary processing to an allowlisted location, prohibit backup/replication until authorized, quarantine expired material from use, and require an independently recorded deletion verification including replicas and restored copies. Restores must recheck expiry before access. Do not claim secure erasure from unlinking or a receipt alone; storage-specific assurance is required. This phase executes no deletion and implements no retention service. Derived aggregates, model outputs, source digests and deletion receipts require their own permitted-retention decisions.
