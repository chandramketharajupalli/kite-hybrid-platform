# Phase1444BTrustedTimePolicy.v1 and Phase1444BRevocationReplayPolicy.v1

Both policies are PROPOSED. Real time status TRUSTED_TIME_UNVERIFIED; durable replay status UNVERIFIED. Local wall time, certificate file creation time and preparation time do not supply independent time authority. Proposed maximum uncertainty is one second and requires policy approval; a signed independent time receipt must bind its source identity, UTC instant, uncertainty, capture/verification provenance and freshness. Availability/freshness policy must be certified before enrollment becomes usable. An offline signed receipt without an independently justified advancing clock is insufficient for continuing authorization.

For later adoption only, expiry = independently verified approval UTC + exactly 168 hours. No timestamp or expiry is assigned now. Validate approval chronology and the entire [now-uncertainty, now+uncertainty] interval: approval <= lower bound, upper bound < expiry. Authority must cover the whole decision validity interval. Reject future-dated approvals, expired decisions, excessive uncertainty, naive timestamps, missing/unauthenticated evidence and clock rollback. Independently anchored monotonic checkpoints must survive restarts and restoration; caller-supplied checkpoints in tests are only simulation.

Explicitly check key revocation, owner/role authority revocation and decision revocation. Bind the current revocation snapshot digest, issuer, monotonic generation and freshness; reject an unavailable/stale/unauthenticated snapshot. No latest-wins rule for conflicting decisions. Require independently authenticated explicit supersession and preserve both records. Unknown conflicts or lost keys deny.

Before executing any future authorized action, atomically validate current policy and consume both a globally unique decision ID and nonce in an approved durable transaction. Include baseline/scope/authority/decision fingerprints, authenticated clock checkpoint, revocation generation and unique constraints. Concurrent consumers must allow at most one commit. Idempotent recovery must never execute twice. Record an append-only hash chain anchored by an independent authority; a local hash chain alone cannot detect restoring the whole store. Fail closed on incomplete write, integrity failure, backend outage, rollback or missing checkpoint. Establish crash/restart/restore and concurrent-consumer evidence before certification.

VolatileFixtureLedger uses a process-local lock and sets only. Its concurrency test demonstrates one-process exclusion; restart safety is explicitly rejected with DURABLE_REPLAY_UNVERIFIED. No SQL store, independent anchor, production revocation service or durable consumption is deployed. Tests of missing mechanisms confirm denial, not the existence of those mechanisms. No pending or synthetic packet can adopt the amendment or authorize an implementation.
