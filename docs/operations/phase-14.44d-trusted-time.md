# Phase1444DTrustedTimeAuthority.v1

TRUSTED_TIME_UNVERIFIED. Evaluate an authenticated timestamp authority or signed time service only after exact authority, enrolled trust anchors, signature format/algorithm, certificate chain policy, revocation evidence and security review are approved. A timestamp token can attest a bound event at issuance; it is not automatically a current-time oracle. Require a challenge/nonce bound to the decision or observation, verified signature/chain/role, issuance UTC, validity and stated uncertainty. Ordinary NTP, local Windows time and a caller-supplied timestamp are insufficient. No service or anchor is selected here.

Proposed synthetic limits: uncertainty at most1000ms and evidence freshness at most300seconds. These limits require independent policy approval; they are not measured capabilities. Production policy must include source uncertainty, transport delay bounds and oscillator drift growth in its interval. Canonical UTC is six fractional digits and Z. Unknown uncertainty or freshness denies. A new clock source cannot reset prior verified monotonic high-water marks. Revocation freshness must cover both source and owner authority; missing/offline-stale revocation denies.

For approval independently verified at t, require expiry exactly t+168hours and not-before=t. The entire justified current-time interval must fit within [t,expiry) and the source-evidence validity interval. Reject future approval, expired/ambiguous interval, rollback, forward jump outside justified tolerance, stale evidence and revoked authority. No countdown starts during preparation. Synthetic tests exercise interval boundaries; they do not validate actual signatures.

Offline continuation requires recently authenticated evidence, conservative elapsed-time/uncertainty accounting and verified continuity. Pure fixture checks bind process, boot and snapshot identities and monotonic origin; real identity/anti-restore attestation is unavailable. Process restart, reboot, snapshot restore, monotonic regression or continuity uncertainty denies until independently refreshed/reconciled. A process-local monotonic counter cannot reconstruct trusted wall time after reboot. Never extend expiry or extrapolate indefinitely. Complete offline unavailability is fail closed with TRUSTED_TIME_UNVERIFIED, not local-clock fallback.
